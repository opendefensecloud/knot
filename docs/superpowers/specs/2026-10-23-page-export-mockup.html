3 · How knot-specific things come out

  • MentionsPlain text with the person's name, e.g. "@Christian Hüning".
  • DatesWritten out as a date, e.g. "14 Nov 2026".
  • Links to pagesClickable links back to the page in knot (colleagues need access to open them).
  • Checklists☐ / ☑ characters, which look the same in Word and LibreOffice.
  • TablesKeep their column widths, merged cells, and lists or images inside cells.
  • DiagramsMermaid diagrams and boards as pictures; if one can't be drawn, its source or name appears instead.
  • AttachmentsFile name and size, linking back to knot.
  • CommentsLeft out.
  • PaperA4, page numbers in the footer.
